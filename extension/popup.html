TrueBeat
Records up to 20 s of the video playing in this tab and sends it only to your backend, which analyses it and then deletes it. The extension keeps nothing.
Checks for AI-generated video (and reads Content Credentials) and, weakly, for face-swaps. The panel on the page shows your backend's verdict, its evidence and its caveat. 'No sign of AI generation' is not proof a video is real.
